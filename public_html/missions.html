The mission Helalia states, and the five values underneath it.

Helalia

A nurturing campus that asks more of every student.

At Helalia we are dedicated to a nurturing, enriching education. The mission is academic excellence, character, and lifelong learning.

01

Excellence

The highest quality of teaching, resources, and support the school can sustain.

02

Integrity

Honesty and ethical behaviour with students, parents, staff, and the city around us.

03

Respect

Every person on campus has a place — abilities, views, and backgrounds included.

04

Collaboration

Students, parents, and teachers work as one table, not three waiting rooms.

05

Innovation

New ways to teach when the old way stops serving the child in front of us.
