Origin

Founded to last.

Helalia Language School was founded in 2008 by Helal Al-Kuwait Company to be one of Alexandria's leading educational institutions. The board consulted specialised educators and invested that experience in HLS.

The mission depends on distinctive, qualified staff who inspire every student to reach full potential in a safe, friendly and adventurous environment where everyone is valued and respected.
